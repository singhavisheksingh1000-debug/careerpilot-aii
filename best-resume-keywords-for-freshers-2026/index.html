Many freshers apply to dozens of jobs but receive very few interview calls. One of the biggest reasons is that their resumes do not contain the right keywords.

In 2026, companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to scan resumes before a recruiter reviews them. If your resume lacks important ATS keywords, it may never reach a hiring manager.

This guide will show you the best resume keywords for freshers, how ATS systems work, and how to improve your ATS Resume Score.

Before applying: Check your resume using the free ATS Resume Checker available on CareerPilot AII.

What Are Resume Keywords?

Resume keywords are words and phrases that recruiters and ATS software use to identify qualified candidates.

These keywords usually come directly from the job description.

Examples:

  • Recruitment
  • Excel
  • SQL
  • Communication Skills
  • Data Analysis
  • Project Management
  • Customer Service

The more relevant keywords you include naturally, the better your ATS score can become.

Why ATS Keywords Matter in 2026

Most organizations receive hundreds of applications.

ATS software helps recruiters:

  • Filter resumes
  • Identify suitable candidates
  • Rank applicants
  • Save hiring time

A resume with strong ATS keywords is more likely to appear near the top of recruiter searches.
