ATS Resume Format for 2026: How to Make a Resume That Gets Interview Calls

Use a simple ATS-friendly resume structure, targeted keywords, and measurable achievements before you apply.

Check your resume free Get the ₹99 HR Toolkit

Updated for 2026 job applications · 9 minute read

Many job seekers apply to dozens of jobs but never receive interview calls. One major reason is that their resume is not easy for Applicant Tracking Systems, also called ATS, to read.

In 2026, companies use ATS and AI screening tools to scan resumes before recruiters read them. These systems usually check your resume structure, keywords, job titles, skills, education, and experience. If your resume has poor formatting or missing keywords, it may get rejected before a human sees it.

That is why an ATS-friendly resume format is important.

What Is an ATS-Friendly Resume?

An ATS-friendly resume is a resume that can be easily read by recruitment software.

It uses:

  • Simple formatting
  • Clear section headings
  • Job-related keywords
  • Clean fonts
  • Standard resume structure
  • Measurable achievements

Your resume should be easy for both software and recruiters to understand.

Best ATS Resume Format for 2026

The best ATS resume format for most job seekers is the reverse chronological format.

This means your latest experience comes first.

Recommended structure:

  1. Name and Contact Information
  2. Professional Summary
  3. Key Skills
  4. Work Experience
  5. Projects
  6. Education
  7. Certifications
  8. Tools and Technologies

This format works well for freshers, experienced professionals, career switchers, HR candidates, data analysts, business analysts, and students.

1. Contact Information

At the top of your resume, include:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Professional email
  • LinkedIn profile
  • Portfolio or GitHub link, if relevant
  • City and country

Avoid adding personal details like marital status, religion, full address, or unnecessary photos unless the job specifically requires them.

Example

Avneet Singh
Kolkata, India
avneetk@email.com
LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/avishek
Portfolio: careerpilot-aii.vercel.app

2. Professional Summary

Your summary should be short and targeted.

Do not write generic lines like:

“Hardworking and passionate candidate looking for growth.”

Instead, write a role-specific summary.

Example for fresher

Commerce graduate with strong interest in HR operations, recruitment coordination, resume screening, and employee documentation. Skilled in Excel, communication, job posting, candidate coordination, and AI-based productivity tools. Seeking an entry-level HR Executive role.

Example for Data Analyst

Entry-level Data Analyst skilled in Excel, SQL, Power BI, data cleaning, dashboard creation, and business reporting. Completed hands-on projects in sales analysis, HR analytics, and customer trend analysis. Looking for a junior data analyst role.

3. Key Skills Section

The skills section is very important for ATS.

Use job-related keywords from the job description.

Do not overfill your resume with fake skills. Add only skills you can explain in an interview.

4. Work Experience

Write your experience using bullet points.

Each bullet should show action, task, and result.

Weak bullet:

Handled recruitment work.

Strong bullet:

Screened 80+ resumes weekly and shortlisted candidates based on job requirements, improving interview scheduling speed.

Use action verbs like:

Managed
Created
Improved
Coordinated
Analyzed
Reduced
Increased
Prepared
Tracked
Supported
Organized

5. Use Measurable Achievements

ATS and recruiters both prefer specific results.

Instead of writing:

Worked on reports.

Write:

Prepared weekly Excel reports for recruitment activity and reduced manual tracking time by 25%.

Instead of:

Helped candidates.

Write:

Coordinated with 50+ candidates for interview scheduling, follow-ups, and documentation.

Numbers make your resume stronger.

6. Projects Section for Freshers

If you are a fresher, your project section can help you stand out.

HR Resume Screening Automation Project

Created an AI-assisted resume screening workflow to identify candidate skills, experience, and role fit. Reduced manual resume review time by 60% using structured prompts and spreadsheet tracking.

Data Analyst Sales Dashboard Project

Built an Excel and Power BI dashboard to analyze monthly sales, product performance, and customer trends. Used pivot tables, charts, and slicers to present business insights.

7. Education Section

Keep education simple.

Example:

M.Com – University Name – 2025
B.Com – University Name – 2023

Freshers can include relevant coursework, but experienced professionals should keep this section short.

8. Certifications

Add certifications that support your target role.

Example:

Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate
LinkedIn Learning Prompt Engineering
Project Management Fundamentals
HR Analytics Course
Excel for Business

Do not add too many unrelated certificates.

9. Best File Format for ATS

For most applications, use PDF if the company allows it.

Use DOCX if the job portal specifically asks for Word format.

Avoid:

  • Images
  • Text boxes
  • Fancy tables
  • Heavy graphics
  • Icons
  • Unusual fonts
  • Two-column layouts

Simple resumes perform better in ATS systems.

Recommended font size:

Name: 16–20

11. ATS Resume Mistakes to Avoid

Avoid these common mistakes:

  • Using a designer resume with too many graphics
  • Adding skills that are not in the job description
  • Using creative section names like “My Journey”
  • Missing LinkedIn profile
  • Writing long paragraphs
  • Not adding measurable results
  • Using the same resume for every job
  • Not matching keywords with the target role

12. How to Match Resume Keywords

Open the job description and find repeated words.

For example, if the job description says:

Recruitment
Candidate screening
Interview coordination
Excel
HR operations
Communication

Then your resume should naturally include these words if they match your real skills.

Do not copy the entire job description. Use keywords naturally.

Final Checklist Before Applying

Before sending your resume, check:

  • Is your resume one or two pages?
  • Does it include target role keywords?
  • Is your email professional?
  • Is your LinkedIn link added?
  • Are skills easy to scan?
  • Are achievements measurable?
  • Is formatting simple?
  • Is the resume customized for this job?

Conclusion

In 2026, a good resume is not only about design. It must be readable by ATS software and impressive for recruiters.

Use a simple format, clear headings, job-related keywords, and measurable achievements. Before applying, always check whether your resume is ATS-friendly.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best ATS resume format for 2026?

The reverse chronological resume format is best for most job seekers because it presents recent experience first and keeps the resume easy for ATS systems and recruiters to scan.

Should I use PDF or DOCX for ATS?

Use PDF when the company allows it, and use DOCX when the job portal specifically asks for a Word document.

Can freshers use this ATS resume format?

Yes. Freshers can use the same structure and strengthen the resume with projects, skills, certifications, and measurable academic or internship achievements.
