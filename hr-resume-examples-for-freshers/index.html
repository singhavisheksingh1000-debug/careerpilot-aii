Why HR Freshers Need an ATS-Friendly Resume

Most companies use ATS software to scan resumes before a recruiter reviews them.

An ATS-friendly resume helps:

  • Pass automated screening systems
  • Match HR job descriptions
  • Improve resume ranking
  • Increase interview opportunities
  • Highlight relevant HR skills

Best ATS Resume Format for HR Freshers

Use this structure when building your fresher HR resume:

1. Contact Information

Include:

  • Full Name
  • Mobile Number
  • Professional Email
  • LinkedIn Profile
  • Location

Example:
Full Name
Phone: +91 XXXXX XXXXX
Email: yourname@email.com
LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/yourname
Location: Kolkata, India

2. Professional Summary

Example: Motivated HR fresher with strong communication skills and knowledge of recruitment, employee onboarding, candidate screening, and HR operations. Proficient in MS Excel, Google Sheets, and AI productivity tools. Seeking an entry-level HR Executive role to contribute to talent acquisition and employee engagement initiatives.

3. Key Skills

Add relevant HR keywords:

  • Recruitment
  • Candidate Screening
  • Interview Coordination
  • Employee Onboarding
  • HR Operations
  • Communication Skills
  • Talent Acquisition
  • MS Excel
  • Google Sheets
  • Documentation Management

4. Education

Example:
Master of Commerce (M.Com)
University Name
2025

Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com)
University Name
2023

5. Projects

Projects help freshers compensate for limited experience.

Recruitment Tracking Project

  • Created an Excel-based candidate tracking system.
  • Improved interview scheduling and follow-up tracking.
  • Used data organization techniques to streamline recruitment processes.

Resume Screening Project

  • Evaluated resumes against job descriptions.
  • Identified keyword matches and candidate suitability.
  • Practiced ATS-style resume analysis.

6. Certifications

Examples:

  • HR Analytics Fundamentals
  • Recruitment and Talent Acquisition
  • Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate
  • LinkedIn Learning HR Courses
  • Prompt Engineering Certification

Common HR Resume Mistakes

Avoid:

  • Generic career objective
  • No measurable achievements
  • Poor formatting
  • Missing keywords
  • No LinkedIn profile
  • Using the same resume for every application
