Resume Checklist

  • Use one clear professional headline.
  • Add measurable achievements where possible.
  • Keep formatting ATS-friendly and simple.
  • Include skills matching the job description.
  • Save your resume as PDF before applying.
💬

HR Interview Practice

  • Tell me about yourself.
  • Why should we hire you?
  • What are your strengths and weaknesses?
  • Why do you want this role?
  • Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

7-Day Action Plan

  • Day 1: Update resume.
  • Day 2: Improve LinkedIn profile.
  • Day 3: Practise HR answers.
  • Day 4: Apply to 10 jobs.
  • Day 5: Follow up professionally.
  • Day 6: Take mock interview.
  • Day 7: Review and improve.
