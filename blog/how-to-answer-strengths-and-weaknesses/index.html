How to Answer Strengths and Weaknesses

Use clear examples to answer a classic interview question.

Updated for practical job preparation · 5 minute read

Use clear examples to answer a classic interview question. This guide gives you a simple structure you can use today without overcomplicating your job search.

Start with the basics

Focus on clarity, relevance, and evidence. Employers want to understand what you can contribute and how you approach your work. Use specific examples wherever possible and keep every point connected to the role you want.

A practical step-by-step approach

  1. Read the job description and highlight the recurring skills.
  2. Choose two or three examples that show those skills in action.
  3. Write a concise first draft using clear, direct language.
  4. Review the draft from a recruiter's perspective.
  5. Practise aloud or ask a trusted person for feedback.
CareerPilot tip: Small focused improvements are more useful than trying to perfect everything at once.

What to avoid

Avoid generic statements, overly complicated wording, and claims without examples. Your preparation should make it easier for a recruiter to see your strengths quickly.
