Interview questions by role

Use these frequently asked questions to structure your preparation and rehearse clear examples.

HR interview questions

  1. Tell me about yourself.
  2. Why do you want to work in HR?
  3. How would you handle an employee conflict?
  4. What is your approach to recruitment?
  5. How do you protect confidential employee information?

Data analyst interview questions

  1. How do you clean a messy dataset?
  2. Explain the difference between INNER JOIN and LEFT JOIN.
  3. Which Excel functions do you use most often?
  4. How do you communicate insights to a non-technical audience?
  5. How do you validate dashboard metrics?

Business analyst interview questions

  1. How do you gather requirements?
  2. How do you manage conflicting stakeholder priorities?
  3. What is a process flow diagram?
  4. How do you document acceptance criteria?
  5. Tell us about a process improvement you suggested.

Project manager interview questions

  1. How do you manage project risks?
  2. How do you handle scope changes?
  3. How do you keep stakeholders informed?
  4. Describe a project that missed a deadline.
  5. Which project metrics do you track?

Fresher interview questions

  1. Tell me about yourself.
  2. Why should we hire you?
  3. What are your strengths and weaknesses?
  4. Describe a college project you are proud of.
  5. Where do you see yourself in three years?
